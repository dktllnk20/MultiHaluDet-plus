# Dataset Availability and Attribution

## Availability

The TriviaQA hard-negative dataset is not publicly available in this repository at the moment.

If you are interested in using the dataset for your research or experiments, feel free to contact me via email. I'd be happy to share it and discuss its use!

## About the Dataset

This dataset is based on [TriviaQA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lucadiliello/triviaqa), with additional hard-negative generation, manual verification, and label corrections carried out as part of this research project.
